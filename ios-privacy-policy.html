Privacy & transparency

Privacy Policy.

How WarpFetch for iOS handles your downloads, files, browsing information, and purchases.

Summary

Privacy at a glance

WarpFetch for iOS is designed to manage transfers and files on your device. WarpFetch does not include an advertising or third-party analytics SDK and does not operate an account or cloud-sync service for your downloads. This does not mean that websites you visit or Apple services never receive information.

Download URLs, browser history and bookmarks, saved files, preferences, and resume information are handled on-device for the app's functions. Visiting a website or fetching a file sends a request to that third-party service. Apple manages App Store payments.

Last updated: October 22, 2026 · App: WarpFetch for iOS (iPhone and iPad), not the separate macOS app.

This policy covers WarpFetch for iOS, support correspondence and this support website. Features available in your installed version may differ. Third-party websites and services have their own privacy practices.

1. Scope and controller

This policy applies to the WarpFetch for iOS application, its user-invoked Share extension, its download manager, in-app web browser, local file operations, and Apple-managed purchase functionality. It does not cover the separate WarpFetch for macOS application.

Contact WarpFetch with questions, privacy requests or complaints at warpdownloader@gmail.com.

2. Information used on your device

To provide the features you request, WarpFetch may process and store the following information locally:

  • Downloads: file URLs, filenames, source hostnames, download history, destinations, file sizes, timestamps, progress and state, retries, task identifiers, and resumability or validation information.
  • Files you choose: downloaded files, copies imported using the Files picker, and the ZIP, extracted-folder, and audio outputs you choose to create.
  • Browser: saved bookmarks, a bounded list of normal-tab recent pages, normal-tab order/selection and sanitized page addresses for restoration, plus website data (such as cookies and cached data) that WebKit stores to make sites function. Private tabs use nonpersistent website data stores and are excluded from saved history, tab restoration and reopening closed tabs. Saved page addresses omit query parameters and fragments; form results and direct-file pages may not be restored.
  • Preferences: download concurrency, Wi-Fi-only, browser homepage and content-blocking options, notification and Live Activity choices, appearance and other settings.
  • Optional local diagnostics: diagnostic builds may provide a user-controlled way to retain and export local diagnostic files. Production builds reviewed for this page do not automatically upload them to WarpFetch-operated servers.

You do not need a WarpFetch account to manage local downloads and files.

HTTP server credentials: if a direct download requires authentication, WarpFetch uses the username and password you provide to authenticate with the relevant server. Credentials may remain in memory during the app session. If you choose “Save in device Keychain,” they are stored in this device's Keychain, associated with the server host and authentication realm. This storage does not enable Keychain synchronization. These credentials are separate from website logins and cookies.

Deletion records: Recently Deleted retains eligible files and their associated download information temporarily to support recovery. Storage preferences control optional automatic deletion.

3. Browsing and downloads

When you enter a URL, visit a site in the built-in browser, use the Share extension, or start a download, WarpFetch handles the corresponding request and its metadata to perform that action.

  • Third-party sites: the destination website, search engine or file host can receive your IP address, request headers, cookies applicable to that site, and other information ordinarily sent in a web request. Those parties have their own privacy policies.
  • Browser sessions: WebKit can retain ordinary site data locally. A site's own tracking, cookies or analytics are not under WarpFetch's control. Optional ad-domain and pop-up blocking may reduce some content but does not promise anonymous browsing.
  • Browser-only downloads: some downloads need website session context such as cookies or headers to function. That context may be sent to the relevant source host as part of the requested transfer.
  • Share extension: when you explicitly choose “Save to WarpFetch” in another app's Share sheet, the shared link or text is handed to WarpFetch using an on-device shared container for validation and download handling. The extension does not continuously watch other apps or their browser traffic.
  • Search: searches typed in WarpFetch's address bar, including private-tab searches, are sent to the search engine chosen under Settings → Browser (Google by default, with DuckDuckGo and Bing available). The search engine is separate from the new-tab homepage. Searches entered within a website go to that website.

Private tabs and shortcuts: private browsing does not conceal your IP address or activity from the website or network operator. Files downloaded in a private tab remain saved. Website shortcuts open third-party websites; they do not provide anonymity or imply affiliation with those services.

4. When data leaves the device

WarpFetch does not send download history, filenames or files to a WarpFetch-operated analytics server. Information can leave your device in these situations:

  • Websites and file servers: to browse pages or download the files you request.
  • Apple: to process or restore in-app purchases, and to provide system services such as notifications, Live Activities, Photos, file providers, or device backup as configured by iOS.
  • Recipients you select: when you share, export, or save a file using a third-party app, cloud provider, or Apple's share sheet.
  • Support report: Settings can prepare a report containing app/system versions, device family and the description you enter. It excludes automatic file lists, URLs, passwords and error logs. You can review it before choosing to share; nothing is sent automatically. Information you type may contain sensitive data, so review it carefully.
  • Support email: when you contact us, your email address and any information you choose to include are received by the support email provider and the developer to respond to your inquiry, investigate reported problems and meet applicable legal obligations.

WarpFetch does not sell download metadata or use it for cross-app tracking. WarpFetch does not control the independent privacy practices of Apple, visited websites, external file providers, or services you choose to share with.

This website: these static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no developer-added analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies or embedded third-party fonts. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes; see GitHub's Privacy Statement. Following an external link or sending email involves the destination service's own processing.

Service providers and location: support email uses Gmail, provided by Google. GitHub, Google, Apple and services you choose may process information outside your country, under their respective policies. Support messages are used for support, security and applicable legal obligations, not advertising. Information may be disclosed when legally required. This policy does not waive rights you have under applicable data-protection law.

5. Permissions and system features

  • Files: the system file picker grants access to the specific items or folders you select. Imported copies are stored in the app's files area.
  • Photos: when you request Save to Photos, iOS provides the authorization and access necessary to write the selected file to your library. Import from Photos uses Apple’s system picker to access only selected items and copies their file representations into app storage; the app does not request broad photo-library read access for this feature.
  • Notifications / Live Activities: you can enable download-completion notifications and progress on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island. Depending on the app's settings, these surfaces may display filenames and transfer progress visible to people near your device.
  • Face ID, Touch ID and passcode: optional App Lock uses Apple's device authentication system. WarpFetch does not receive or store your biometric scans.

You can manage permissions and Live Activities in iOS Settings and the available controls inside WarpFetch.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

WarpFetch Pro for iOS may be offered as an auto-renewing yearly subscription or a non-consumable Lifetime unlock. Purchases, billing, refunds, renewals, and subscription management are handled through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. WarpFetch uses available transaction and entitlement information to determine access to purchased features; it does not collect your payment card number.

Purchases of WarpFetch for iOS and WarpFetch for macOS are separate. You can manage the yearly subscription using Apple's subscription management and use Restore Purchases in WarpFetch Settings. Deleting the app does not by itself cancel an auto-renewing subscription.

7. Storage, retention and security

Download records, normal browser history/bookmarks and saved tabs, preferences (including playback speed), resume-related information, imported copies, and generated outputs are generally kept in the app's on-device storage until removed through its controls, iOS's storage management, or app removal, subject to how iOS and your backup settings handle that data. Background transfers may also use system-managed temporary storage.

Normal WebKit website data may persist until cleared through available system or application controls. Private tabs use nonpersistent website data, but private browsing does not hide your connection from a website or network operator.

We use iOS sandboxing and system-managed permissions and authentication mechanisms. No electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed perfectly secure; download only from sources you trust.

When you email support, correspondence is retained as needed to respond to the request, address follow-up issues and meet applicable obligations; you may request deletion as described below.

Recently Deleted: eligible completed downloads deleted from app storage may remain recoverable for the selected retention period (7, 30 or 90 days; default 30), unless you permanently delete them first. Cleanup runs when the app performs it, so this is not an exact background-deletion deadline. Recently Deleted is not a backup.

Optional Auto-delete: off by default, this moves eligible completed downloads in app storage to Recently Deleted after the selected age (30, 90 or 180 days). It does not apply to imports or external destinations. External file providers control their own retention and recovery.

Saved credentials: Keychain items are stored separately from app documents and browser data. Do not rely on uninstalling the app to remove them; use Saved HTTP Credentials in Settings and check that the entry is removed. Contact support if an entry remains. App Lock protects entry to the app using device authentication; it does not secure files already shared elsewhere.

8. Your choices, controls and deletion

  • Delete individual or multiple downloaded, imported, or exported files using WarpFetch's Files and Downloads controls. These controls act on the specific items and locations described in the app.
  • Close normal tabs to remove them from the saved tab session; recently closed normal tabs remain available in memory until the app session ends. Private tabs are never added to this list.
  • Remove individual bookmarks or clear Recent Pages using the browser's saved-pages controls. Removing a recorded item may not clear every cookie or cache entry maintained separately by WebKit.
  • Disable notifications, Live Activities, filename display, content blocking and App Lock using WarpFetch or system settings, as applicable.
  • You can remove the app and its on-device documents through iOS, taking care to export files you want to keep first. Files previously shared or saved to another destination must be managed there separately.
  • For information voluntarily sent to support, request access, correction or deletion at warpdownloader@gmail.com. Include enough information to identify your email conversation; please do not send passwords or private URLs.

There is no WarpFetch-operated iOS account database to delete. Deleting local content does not cancel Apple subscriptions or erase copies that you shared to external services.

Clear website data: Browser → options → Clear Site Data removes the selected normal-browser categories: cookies/sessions, cache and local website storage. This can sign you out or disrupt authenticated downloads. Bookmarks, recent-page history, private tabs, files and HTTP credentials are separate.

Manage stored passwords: open Settings → Privacy → Saved HTTP Credentials to delete saved entries. Deleting a password here does not revoke it on the remote server; change it with that service if needed.

Your privacy rights: depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or other available rights concerning information held by the developer, and raise a complaint with the appropriate authority. Email the contact below; only information reasonably needed to identify and process your request will be requested. Legal retention obligations may limit deletion. Local-only files are managed on your device; requests about data held independently by Apple, GitHub or another service should also be directed to that provider. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it, subject to applicable law and the effect on the requested feature.

9. Children’s privacy

WarpFetch for iOS is a general-purpose file utility, not designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the support email address inappropriately, please contact us so we can review it.
