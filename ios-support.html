Help us diagnose it

What to include

  • WarpFetch app version and iOS version.
  • What you tried, what you expected, and any visible error.
  • Whether the link was added manually, shared from another app, or opened in WarpFetch's browser.
  • Whether the problem happens on Wi-Fi, cellular, or both.

Privacy tip: Please don't email passwords, cookies, authorization headers, or private signed download links. A website hostname is usually enough initially.

Get started

  1. Open Downloads and tap + to add a direct HTTPS file URL, or use the built-in Browser to find a file link.
  2. Review the download details, location, and network preference. Your first three successfully completed individual downloads are free. After the third completion, new downloads require WarpFetch Pro. Eligible Apple Accounts can choose the 7-day Yearly introductory trial, subscribe yearly, or purchase Lifetime; nothing starts until you confirm with Apple.
  3. Track progress in Downloads. Find completed files under Files → Downloaded.
  4. Use Files → Imported to work with files you select in Apple's Files picker, and Files → Exports for ZIP and audio results.
Background downloads: Direct file transfers generally use iOS background downloads. Some browser-only transfers require WarpFetch to stay open; closing the app from the app switcher can interrupt transfers.

Frequently asked questions

How do the three free downloads work?

Each installation includes three individual downloads before Pro is required. A free allowance is used only after a download completes successfully. An unfinished download reserves a place so multiple simultaneous starts cannot exceed the allowance. Pausing or retrying the same download does not count it twice. A cancelled or deleted unfinished download releases its reservation. Batch Links, Custom Folders, Scheduling and Priority, and eligible multi-connection Fast Downloads are Pro tools from the start. Importing files and playing local media do not use the download allowance.

Does the seven-day trial start after my third download?

No. After the third free download completes, WarpFetch shows the available Pro plans, but it does not start a trial or purchase automatically. If Apple says your Apple Account is eligible, you can choose the 7-day free introductory trial for Yearly Pro and confirm it in Apple's purchase sheet. Otherwise Apple shows the applicable Yearly price. Lifetime Pro is a separate one-time purchase and has no trial.

How do I use website shortcuts?

Use the grid button in the Browser toolbar, or Quick Access on the WarpFetch Start Page. Tapping a shortcut opens that website in the current tab. Choose Settings → Browser → New Tab Homepage to change the home page. Choose Settings → Browser → Search Engine to use Google, DuckDuckGo or Bing independently of the homepage. Shortcuts are not endorsements or download authorizations.

Can I recover a deleted file or turn off automatic deletion?

Open Settings → Storage → Recently Deleted for eligible completed downloads deleted from app storage. Restore before the selected retention period expires; permanently deleted items cannot be restored through WarpFetch. Auto-delete is off by default; if enabled, you can turn it off in Settings → Storage. Imports and external destinations are excluded from automatic cleanup. External providers have their own recovery rules. Keep backups.

How do tab restoration and bookmarks work?

Normal tabs restore after relaunch, with inactive restored pages loading when selected. Private tabs are never saved or reopened. Saved URLs omit parameters and fragments; form results and direct-file pages may reopen as empty tabs. In Tabs, use the context menu to reorder tabs or Reopen Closed Tab for recent normal tabs. Saved Pages supports bookmark search, add, edit and reorder.

Can I import Photos or use cloud providers?

Files → Imported → Import from Photos copies selected images/videos using Apple's system picker. Originals remain in Photos. Import from Files supports enabled iOS file providers, including iCloud Drive and installed/configured Google Drive or Dropbox providers. Direct account sign-in and automatic cloud synchronization are not implemented.

Can I change playback speed or set a sleep timer?

Local audio/video in Downloaded, Imported and Exports uses the shared player when recognized as media. iOS determines codec support. Speed is remembered without autoplay. Choose a 15–90 minute sleep timer to pause playback; closing the player cancels the timer. Resume positions currently use download IDs, so imported-file resume persistence is not yet equivalent.

How do I prepare a support report?

Settings → Help & About → Create Support Report lets you describe the issue and review app/system versions and device family before sharing. No report is sent automatically. Do not put passwords, private links or sensitive content in the description.

How do I clear browser data or saved server passwords?

Browser → options → Clear Site Data lets you select normal-browser cookies/sessions, cache and local storage. Clearing cookies can sign you out and interrupt authenticated downloads. Clear Recent Pages and remove bookmarks separately from the saved-pages screen. Manage HTTP server credentials under Settings → Privacy → Saved HTTP Credentials; these passwords are separate from browser cookies. If a deleted credential remains listed, contact support without sending the password.

How do free trials, renewal and refunds work?

For an eligible Apple Account, the Yearly plan may include a 7-day free introductory trial. Apple shows the trial, renewal price, and billing terms before you confirm. The subscription begins only after your confirmation and renews automatically until cancelled. If you do not want a trial to renew, Apple advises cancelling at least 24 hours before it ends. Manage it in your Apple Account's Subscriptions settings. Request eligible Apple-billed refunds through Apple's Report a Problem service. Refund eligibility is determined under Apple's rules and applicable law; WarpFetch support cannot cancel an Apple subscription or guarantee a refund.

Where can I read the terms?

Open Settings → Legal → Terms of Use in the app, or read the latest published version on this website. Check the version date because the app can contain an earlier bundled copy. Apple's Standard EULA governs the software license unless a different controlling license is validly provided; the WarpFetch notices supplement it.

Why won't a download resume at the same byte?

Resuming depends on the transfer method, available iOS resume data, and the server's behavior. Some failures require a fresh download. WarpFetch indicates whether it will try to resume or start again, but the result can change if the server rejects an existing resume token.

Why is my download waiting?

Check whether Wi-Fi Only is enabled under Settings, whether the connection is available, and whether the active download limit has been reached. Additional direct downloads may wait in a queue until a slot opens.

Can I keep downloading when the app is closed?

Eligible direct downloads use Apple's background transfer system. Background execution and delivery are controlled by iOS. Browser-based transfers that depend on a webpage or login session may not continue reliably when WarpFetch is suspended; force-quitting can interrupt ongoing transfers.

How do I play a downloaded or imported file in the background?

Open Files in WarpFetch (or select Files in the iPad sidebar), choose Downloaded, Imported, or Exports, and tap a supported audio or video file. Press Play, then go to the Home Screen. With App Lock turned off, audio continues and WarpFetch publishes system Now Playing controls on the Lock Screen and in Control Center. If App Lock is enabled, leaving WarpFetch closes the player for privacy. Closing the player also stops playback and clears Now Playing. Background audio is for user-started local media playback; it is not used to keep downloads running.

Where are my files?

Open Files in WarpFetch to switch among Downloaded, Imported and Exports. App-owned documents are also exposed through Apple's Files app where supported. Files moved or deleted outside WarpFetch may no longer open from their previous history record.

Which archive and media operations are supported?

You can create ZIP archives and extract supported ZIP files. For audio extraction, first download or import a local MP4, MOV, or M4V video that contains a readable audio track. In Files, open Downloaded or Imported, use the file's menu, and choose Extract Audio to M4A. The result appears in Exports. WarpFetch cannot extract audio from a silent or unsupported file, a website stream, or DRM-protected content. RAR and 7z extraction aren't currently supported.

Can I download any video or music from any website?

No. WarpFetch is intended for direct files you are authorized to download. It does not support platform media extraction, streaming capture or DRM bypass. A website shortcut is only a navigation link, not download permission or a promise of supported media downloads. Only save, transform or share content when you have the required rights and authorization from the applicable service.

Why isn't download progress visible on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island?

Enable Live Activities in iOS Settings and WarpFetch Settings. iOS controls when these surfaces appear and how frequently they update; progress shown while the app is suspended may be the last reported value.

How do I restore my Pro purchase or manage a subscription?

Open WarpFetch → Settings → Restore Purchases. For a yearly subscription, use Apple's Manage Subscriptions page to review or cancel renewal. A Lifetime purchase is a one-time iOS unlock for the supported lifetime of the app; it does not cancel an existing Yearly subscription. Cancel Yearly separately to avoid renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. WarpFetch for iOS purchases are separate from the macOS app's purchases.

Does cancelling the yearly plan remove my files?

Cancellation itself does not delete saved files. When the paid entitlement expires, Pro tools are locked and new individual downloads are available only if a free-download allowance remains. The browser and previously saved files remain available. Your enabled storage-cleanup settings and any deletion actions continue to apply. Keep independent backups.

Does WarpFetch require an account or sign-in?

No. WarpFetch has no app-level account, cloud-sync login, or demo account. A sign-in page shown inside the built-in browser belongs to the third-party website you opened. Saved HTTP server credentials, if you explicitly choose to save them, are stored in the device Keychain and are separate from website logins.

How do I report a suspected security or privacy issue?

Email warpdownloader@gmail.com with a description, avoiding secrets and private URLs. See the iOS Privacy Policy for how data is handled.

About WarpFetch for iOS

WarpFetch is an iPhone and iPad download and file manager with direct HTTPS downloads, an in-app browser, local file organization, system sharing, ZIP operations, local audio/video playback with background audio, supported local video-to-M4A extraction, and optional Live Activities. Features can vary with iOS permissions, connectivity, file providers, media formats, and whether Pro is enabled.

WarpFetch for macOS is a separate app and purchase. Read the iOS Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
