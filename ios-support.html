Help us diagnose it

What to include

  • WarpFetch app version and iOS version.
  • What you tried, what you expected, and any visible error.
  • Whether the link was added manually, shared from another app, or opened in WarpFetch's browser.
  • Whether the problem happens on Wi-Fi, cellular, or both.

Privacy tip: Please don't email passwords, cookies, authorization headers, or private signed download links. A website hostname is usually enough initially.

Get started

  1. Open Downloads and tap + to add a direct HTTPS file URL, or use the built-in Browser to find a file link.
  2. Review the download details, location, and network preference; then start the transfer.
  3. Track progress in Downloads. Find completed files under Files → Downloaded.
  4. Use Files → Imported to work with files you select in Apple's Files picker, and Files → Exports for ZIP and audio results.
Background downloads: Direct file transfers generally use iOS background downloads. Some browser-only transfers require WarpFetch to stay open; closing the app from the app switcher can interrupt transfers.

Frequently asked questions

Why won't a download resume at the same byte?

Resuming depends on the transfer method, available iOS resume data, and the server's behavior. Some failures require a fresh download. WarpFetch indicates whether it will try to resume or start again, but the result can change if the server rejects an existing resume token.

Why is my download waiting?

Check whether Wi-Fi Only is enabled under Settings, whether the connection is available, and whether the active download limit has been reached. Additional direct downloads may wait in a queue until a slot opens.

Can I keep downloading when the app is closed?

Eligible direct downloads use Apple's background transfer system. Background execution and delivery are controlled by iOS. Browser-based transfers that depend on a webpage or login session may not continue reliably when WarpFetch is suspended; force-quitting can interrupt ongoing transfers.

Where are my files?

Open Files in WarpFetch to switch among Downloaded, Imported and Exports. App-owned documents are also exposed through Apple's Files app where supported. Files moved or deleted outside WarpFetch may no longer open from their previous history record.

Which archive and media operations are supported?

You can create ZIP archives, extract supported ZIP files, and export M4A audio from supported local videos (such as MP4, MOV and M4V). RAR and 7z extraction aren't currently supported. You can share or preview supported files using iOS features.

Can I download any video or music from any website?

No. WarpFetch is intended for direct files you are authorized to download. It does not bypass DRM or guarantee downloads from video-streaming platforms, protected websites, or webpages that do not expose a usable file.

Why isn't download progress visible on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island?

Enable Live Activities in iOS Settings and WarpFetch Settings. iOS controls when these surfaces appear and how frequently they update; progress shown while the app is suspended may be the last reported value.

How do I restore my Pro purchase or manage a subscription?

Open WarpFetch → Settings → Restore Purchases. For a yearly subscription, use Apple's Manage Subscriptions page to review or cancel renewal. A Lifetime purchase is a one-time unlock. WarpFetch for iOS purchases are separate from the macOS app's purchases.

Does cancelling the yearly plan remove my files?

No. Subscription changes affect access to Pro features; locally saved files are not automatically deleted by a cancellation. Availability of specific Pro tools is determined by the current entitlement.

How do I report a suspected security or privacy issue?

Email warpdownloader@gmail.com with a description, avoiding secrets and private URLs. See the iOS Privacy Policy for how data is handled.

About WarpFetch for iOS

WarpFetch is an iPhone download and file manager with direct downloads, an in-app browser, local file organization, system sharing, ZIP operations, and optional Live Activities. Features can vary with iOS permissions, connectivity, file providers, and whether Pro is enabled.

Using WarpFetch for Mac? Visit the separate macOS support page. For privacy information, see the iOS Privacy Policy.
